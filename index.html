    VizMLIR / field guide

    See what your MLIR pass changed.

    VizMLIR turns two MLIR snapshots into a graph and a focused structural diff. Everything runs in your browser; your IR stays local.

    Start here

    • Open Workspace and paste the earlier IR into Baseline.
    • Paste the lowered or transformed IR into Current.
    • Use the graph to inspect operation relationships and the pass diff to find added, removed, and changed operations.

    Try a small example

    This example creates a tensor, fills it, and multiplies it. The graph turns each operation into a node and draws edges for SSA data flow.

    Then change linalg.fill and compare.

    How to read the visual

    Follow the arrows from values being produced to operations that consume them. Click a node to see its label and parent. The pass diff on the right highlights what was added, removed, or changed between the two snapshots.
