        About pass timing

        From the log's -mlir-timing report. Nested passes show one total across every op they ran on. mlir-opt does not measure memory per pass; peak memory is the whole process, read from /usr/bin/time output when the log includes it.

        No timing report in this log. Add -mlir-timing to the mlir-opt command, and wrap it in /usr/bin/time -l (macOS) or /usr/bin/time -v (Linux) for peak memory.

        About buffers

        Buffers the IR allocates (memref.alloc, memref.alloca, gpu.alloc), baseline against current. Each bar is a buffer's live range through its function, top to bottom in the source: from the allocation to its dealloc, to the end when it is returned (marked edge), or else to its last use. A use inside a loop keeps it live until the loop ends. Sizes are static; buffers with dynamic shapes are counted but not sized. Peak live is the largest function's. Click a current buffer to show its line.

          About symbol history

          Every function, kernel, GPU module and global in the trace, with the passes that created it, changed its body, lowered it to another op (func.func → llvm.func), or removed it. Nested symbols are named as in gpu.launch_func (@module::@kernel), and a change inside a nested symbol is not counted against its parent. Click a pass to go to it, or a symbol to follow it step by step.
